SLIDE 02 · THE PROBLEM

Existing AI Assistants Are Broken

Smart speakers and AI tools are siloed, cloud-dependent, and dumb about the physical world.

🔒
Cloud Lock-In & Privacy Risk
Alexa, Google Home, and Siri send every word you speak to corporate servers. You have zero control over your data, and a single internet outage renders your "smart" home completely useless.
🧱
No Physical World Integration
Existing AI tools can set timers and play music. They cannot control a servo motor, stream from an IP camera, scan your local network for new devices, or route audio through different hardware in real-time.
📦
Single-Platform, Single-Device Silos
Your phone AI doesn't know about your desktop. Your desktop client can't hear your home's microphone. Every device operates in complete isolation with no unified state or fallback intelligence.
💸
Expensive Proprietary Ecosystems
To get a "smart" home, you must buy vendor-specific hubs, pay monthly subscriptions, and accept incompatible ecosystems that fight each other. A $400 investment that's obsolete in 2 years.

SLIDE 03 · THE SOLUTION

Introducing Project MEKA

A fully decentralized, open-source AI ecosystem that lives in your hardware, your phone, your browser — everywhere simultaneously.

🧠
AI ORCHESTRATION

Gemini-powered natural language AI understands intent and maps it directly to physical hardware actions — LED controls, servo movements, camera snapshots.

📡
DYNAMIC FALLBACK

If the physical microphone breaks, MEKA auto-routes to your phone. If the speaker fails, it switches to Bluetooth. Hardware failure is invisible to the user.

🌐
ZERO CLOUD DEPENDENCY

Runs fully on LAN. Transitioning to local Llama 3 + Whisper for 100% offline operation. Your voice stays on your hardware, period.

📷
PHONE AS BODY NODE

Turn any smartphone into a camera, microphone, and speaker node for MEKA — via the Phone Bridge. No extra hardware required.

🔐
BIOMETRIC SECURITY

Fingerprint/FaceID gates destructive commands. Voice-print recognition ensures only the owner can trigger physical actions. One-time MAC-based device permissions.

⚡
50ms STATE SYNC

Firebase Realtime Database synchronizes all client states in under 50 milliseconds. Turn on a LED from Telegram and watch the Webapp UI update instantly.
